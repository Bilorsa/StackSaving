# StackSave PoE demonstration checklist

1. Register and log in.
2. Show masked password input and explain Firebase Auth manages credentials when connected.
3. Open Settings and change English/isiZulu.
4. Toggle notifications and offline mode.
5. Disconnect network and show cached deals.
6. Reconnect and refresh; REST feed is fetched and cached.
7. Tap Test notification.
8. Explain Google SSO and real-time FCM are scaffolded as POE-only and need provider/server configuration.
9. Demonstrate on a physical Android phone and record the required voice-over.
